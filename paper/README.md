# LiSA paper (CVPR 2027 submission draft)

"LiSA: Light-Space Atlases for Relightable Gaussian Splatting" — paper on the final LiSA-staged
method of PORT-GS (`representation light_atlas`, 30k+8k staged recipe) and the supplementary
experiments of 2026-10-05/06.

## Layout

```text
paper/
├── README.md            this file
├── template/            official CVPR author kit as downloaded (main branch + CVPR2026-v1 tag zips)
├── latex/               paper source
│   ├── main.tex         main paper (review mode, anonymous)
│   ├── supp.tex         supplementary material (compiled separately, cross-refs to main)
│   ├── preamble.tex     kit preamble + placeholder macros (\torun, \TBD)
│   ├── sec/             sections 0-6
│   ├── fig/             figure environments (TikZ overview in fig/pipeline_tikz.tex)
│   ├── tables/          GENERATED by scripts/make_tables.py — do not edit by hand
│   ├── figures/         GENERATED raster panels (scripts/make_figures.py)
│   ├── main.bib         bibliography
│   └── cvpr.sty, ieeenat_fullname.bst   (from the kit)
├── scripts/             figure/table generation and evaluation-only diagnostics (read-only on results)
├── data/                derived numbers (numbers.json, transfer_shares.json, light_novelty.json)
│                        and the read-only diagnostic renders used by figures (renders/)
├── codex/pipeline/      first draft of the overview figure produced with the local Codex CLI
├── reviews/             self-review, three mock CVPR reviews, revision log
└── build/               compiled PDFs (main.pdf, supp.pdf) and LaTeX logs
```
