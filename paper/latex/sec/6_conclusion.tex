\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Rasterizing Gaussians from a point light records the fraction of flux each primitive intercepts, and \ours turns this deposit into a learned atlas that gives every receiver a filtered, refinable shadow test and a transfer term, for one extra rasterization per light.
With a radiometrically staged optimization, it is the most consistent method across three benchmarks at a fraction of the usual training time; whether its light-relative design pays off for unseen light directions is the question our pending experiments address.
